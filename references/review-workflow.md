# Evidence-grounded paper review

Read the supplied paper and relevant supplementary material with an available
PDF/text/visual reader. Preserve page, section, equation, figure and table locators.
Report missing sections or unreadable figures; an abstract is not a full-paper
review. Use rendered figures for visual claims and timed media for demo claims.

## Evidence and analytical lenses

Extract the contribution, task/observation/intervention, method, data, baselines,
metrics, key results and limits. Check requested anchors against actual sources.
Use [role-templates.md](role-templates.md) for useful domain, algorithm, experiment
or systems lenses. Select relevant independent lenses rather than a fixed count.
When subagents are unavailable, apply them in the main thread. Give delegated
reviewers accessible artifact paths and narrow questions; use excerpts only when
they cannot access the source, and declare resulting coverage limits.

Verify every major criticism against the paper before synthesis. Agreement among
reviewers is not evidence of truth; preserve substantive disagreements. For
negative claims, search the relevant sections/supplement before saying something
is absent. Distinguish a critical missing experiment from a feasible rebuttal
request: a genuine validity problem need not be fixable within a short rebuttal.

## Venue and output

Use the supplied/current venue form and contribution type. Read
[venue-formats.md](venue-formats.md) for orientation, but verify the current scale,
fields and deadlines when needed. Without a venue, give a conventional review
without inventing a numeric scale.

Deliver the complete requested draft. Field-by-field approval is an option only
when the user requests that collaboration style. Review writing does not authorize
submission, posting, correspondence or repository writes.

Include summary, supported strengths, major/minor weaknesses, questions,
recommendation and confidence where the form requests them. Choose counts from
the evidence. Keep exact numbers when they clarify a material point. Calibrate
score and tone to the evidence; a user-supplied target is not evidence for a score.
Do not promise a score increase regardless of what the rebuttal shows.

Use concise academic prose with precise locations and actionable corrections.
Avoid formulaic wording without deleting necessary uncertainty or technical terms.
Before delivery verify the source locations, required fields, criticism fairness,
rebuttal feasibility, and score/confidence/tone consistency. An independent
`review-review` pass is useful for a complex/full review; a small field edit needs
only the relevant checks. Do not ask for redundant permission to complete the
review already requested.
