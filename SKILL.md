# Paper Review

Read `references/review-workflow.md` for the venue-aware review rubric and
`references/role-templates.md` only when independent expert lenses are useful.

1. Extract claims, methods, experimental contract, evidence, and limitations.
2. Run independent lenses when available, keeping source facts separate from
   reviewer inference.
3. Cross-check every major criticism against the paper and calibrate score,
   confidence, and tone.
4. Format with `references/venue-formats.md` when a venue is specified.
5. Verify the review against the source before delivery. For substantial reviews,
   use the `review-review` rubric, independently when available; a narrow edit
   needs only the relevant checks.

Never invent missing experiments, citations, or field requirements. Identify
what is absent and why it matters.
